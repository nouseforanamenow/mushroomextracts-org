Excluded from the extract comparison, and why

  • Host Defense — Mycelium grown on brown rice. Guarantees “over 55% polysaccharides” and discloses no beta-glucan figure. Polysaccharide totals include the grain starch, so they are not comparable to a beta-glucan percentage.
  • Om Mushrooms — Mycelial biomass and gummies, not a concentrated extract.

Not yet scored: Four Sigmatic, FreshCap (Thrive), Double Wood, Nutricost, Toniiq, Nammex-supplied private labels. These are extract-labelled products whose active-compound testing we have not been able to confirm from a published COA. They enter the tables when that documentation is in hand, with whatever it shows.
